THE ADDA HANDBOOK

How to Play

Two teams. One secret word map. Avoid the Assassin!

For Players / Guessers

  1. Join a team. Enter the room code, your name, and choose Red or Blue. Select Player / Guesser.
  2. Wait for your team's clue. Your Spymaster gives a one-word clue and a number showing how many words it connects to.
  3. Discuss and guess. On your team's turn, tap a word on your phone to reveal it. You can guess up to the clue number plus one, or choose End guessing / pass.
  4. Watch the colors. Your team's card helps you. An opposing team's card helps them. A gray neutral card ends your turn.
  5. Avoid the Assassin! The one black Assassin card ends the round immediately. Your opponents win that round.
  6. Win the round. Reveal all your team's words before the other team does. The championship scoreboard counts rounds won, not individual cards.

Only a regular player on the active team can select cards, and only after a clue is submitted. Players cannot see the secret key.

For Spymasters

  1. Claim your role. Choose a team and select Spymaster. Only one Spymaster is allowed per team.
  2. Study the secret board. Red and blue borders identify team words; gray borders identify neutral words. The black card with a skull and ASSASSIN label is the dangerous word.
  3. Give a clue on your turn. Enter a single-word clue and a number from 1–9. Your clue cannot be an exact word already on the board.
  4. Keep the key secret. Never show your private screen or point at specific words. Let your teammates discuss and choose.
  5. Guide with clues, not reactions. Do not confirm or deny guesses with gestures or extra hints.
  6. Know the risk. If your team selects the Assassin, your team loses that round immediately. The opposing team receives the round win.

Spymasters can see the Assassin before it is guessed, but cannot select word cards themselves.
